### Looking Beyond

Note: Tonight is about looking past the immediate "toil" and into the 5-10 year horizon.

---
### The 10-Year Horizon
- **Death of the Interface**: From clicking buttons to stating Intent.
- **Conductors of Agent Swarms**: Managing "Crews" of AI agents.
- **Billable Time to Billable Value**: The new economy of impact.

Note: Provocative thought: "If our user interface disappeared tomorrow, does our business still have value?" We are moving to a world where software uses software, and our role shifts from "Executors" to "Conductors." We will stop selling our time and start selling our unique human value and the outcomes we orchestrate.
