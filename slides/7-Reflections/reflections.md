### The Augmentation Path
1. **Define**: Audit your current skills.
2. **Augment Core**: Speed up your existing work.
3. **Augment Adjacent**: Do the job of the person next to you.
4. **Augment Next-Adjacent**: Move one step further (e.g. Dev to Architect).
5. **Integrate**: Become a multi-modal professional.

Note: This isn't about replacement; it's about capacity. By augmenting your core, you create the space to learn adjacent skills, eventually becoming a conductor of agentic swarms rather than just an executor.
