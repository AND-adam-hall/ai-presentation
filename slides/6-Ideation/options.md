### Agentic AI
- Linking tools together
- Autonomous workflows

Note: We are shifting from "Assisted Work" (humans using AI tools) to "Agentic Work" (AI agents using software tools). Agents act as the "glue" between systems. We use Orchestration Patterns like "Fan-Out/Fan-In" for parallel processing and "Event-Driven" workflows that trigger based on system states rather than manual clicks.

---
### Bringing Ideas to Life
- Taming Toil (Quick Wins)
- The Value vs. Readiness Matrix

Note: Our primary strategy for quick wins is "Taming Toil"—delegating the low-value, repetitive administrative tasks that slow us down (e.g., summarizing meetings, initial draft generation). We prioritize these using the "Value vs. Readiness Matrix," focusing on High Value/High Readiness opportunities first. Ask yourself: "What repetitive work is slowing my team down?"
