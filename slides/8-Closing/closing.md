### Building the AI Backlog
- **Value vs. Readiness Matrix**
- Prioritize: Quick Wins vs. Strategic Bets
- Scoring with the RICE Framework

Note: To move from ideation to realization, we use a simple filter.
- High Value/High Readiness = Quick Wins (Do First).
- High Value/Low Readiness = Strategic Bets (Fix data, then build).
- Low Value = The Bin or Deprioritize.
We use AI to estimate Reach, Impact, and Effort, but only humans can provide the 'Confidence' score.
